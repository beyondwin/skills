# Reader-directed writing comparison

The user requested continued improvement until superiority is established. This is
authorization to improve and measure, not a reason to force a positive conclusion.
The installed 0.2.2 remains unchanged until a replacement passes the declared gate.

## Change hypothesis

Earlier runs exposed repeated summaries, fragmented procedural conditions, and
verbose or awkward Korean even after factual repairs. Candidate 0.3.0 replaces
generic paragraph advice with a small planning decision: identify the reader's
question, the answer, and the few relationships needed to justify or act on it.
Details get a location according to that purpose, rather than becoming an inventory.
Keep operational and numerical preservation rules. No new runtime model calls.

Alternatives: leave 0.2.2 installed; add more prohibitions; use a mandatory second
model/editor. The latter two add constraints or cost without demonstrated benefit.

## Scope and independence

Twelve new synthetic tasks, four each generated by native Codex, Claude CLI Opus,
and Cursor Agent Grok Medium. Each host receives one explanation, one procedure,
one handoff, and one measurement interpretation. Compare no writing skill,
frozen 0.2.2, and candidate 0.3.0. This is twelve tasks, not 36 independent cases.
The author creates the tasks after fixing the candidate, before seeing responses.
This is an author-designed holdout, not an independently sampled benchmark.

Each trio is evaluated by another provider in two cyclic label orders. The
candidate, incumbent, and baseline each occupy each label four times per order.
Each draft moves to a different label in the second order. Evaluators see the
source and request, but no version, skill, runtime, or mapping. Absolute archive
prefixes are replaced with `/source/` before judging. No prose editing is allowed.

Comparisons: candidate versus no skill, candidate versus incumbent. A per-task
win requires both orderings to prefer the candidate in that pair; a loss requires
both to prefer the comparator. All disagreements, ties, or missing judgments are
ties. Candidate source-fidelity errors veto adoption regardless of preference.
Inspect reported errors against the source; publish author adjudication separately
and do not rewrite the reviewers' raw decisions.

## Fixed decision

Require all twelve generation trios and both judgments per trio. For each of the
two comparisons, require at least eight concordant wins, more wins than losses,
and the exact one-sided fair-coin tail probability on decisive tasks <= 0.025.
The two comparisons share a 0.05 error allowance by equal allocation. These are
task-level model-preference results conditional on this sample, not proof about
all technical writing or human comprehension. Repeated label orders are not extra
samples. No candidate material source errors, no observed regression on structured
output or Git scope, and successful native installation smoke are required.

Two deterministic scorer controls contain a known changed threshold and an exact
duplicate paragraph. Both providers must identify the factual mutation and prefer
the faithful concise draft over its duplicate. No claim based on a failed scorer.

Log length, wall time, and provider-reported usage as secondary measures; shorter
is not intrinsically better. Baseline receives the same request and source.
Reserve at most four retries for transport failures only, with identical input;
never replace a completed unfavorable answer. Primary analysis uses first complete
answer, and missingness and retries remain visible.

## Bound and next decision

36 generations + 24 evaluations + 2 controls + 3 native regressions + 1 installed
smoke + at most 4 transport retries = 70 CLI dispatches. The smoke is conditional
on the adoption gate. No early positive stopping. No host/model fallback.
After the frozen batch, failure means superiority not established. Another version
must use a new evaluation batch and explicitly retain this failed result; do not
continue sampling this test set until it passes. If the candidate is dominated or
the task scores remain at ceiling, keep the incumbent and report the limit.

Raw prompts, responses, provider receipts, and temporary authentication stay outside
Git. Public artifacts contain synthetic tasks, hashes, aggregate measures, and
bounded author conclusions. Codex uses fresh HOME/CODEX_HOME and no memories;
Opus and Grok use the previously audited isolated runners and inline skill text.
This does not establish native Claude/Cursor skill discovery or host support.
