# Personal plain-language revision

The reader found all three earlier pairs hard to distinguish and the terminology
unnatural in both arms. This study targets that defect. It does not reuse the old
superiority claim or silently relax that study's statistical threshold.

0.4.0 builds on the uninstalled 0.3.2 correctness repairs and changes terminology
handling: concrete actors and actions, familiar words, local explanations of
unfamiliar names, no mechanical translation or loss of technical distinctions.
The incumbent is the installed 0.2.2. Trigger and change-message contracts stay fixed.
This remains a personal pilot outside the three supported products.

## Before-run decision

Six cases, two per writer: three known development cases from the reader's sheet
and three fresh author-written transfer cases. The lease prompt says processing
deadline rather than lease; both arms receive this same prompt. Source facts stay
unchanged. New Korean source notes complement the earlier English fixtures; this
is not an experiment identifying source-language effects.

Each case is generated once per arm (12 writes). A different provider judges each
pair twice with A/B reversed (12 judgments). Two orders are one case, not two
independent votes. A win requires both orders to prefer the same arm. Missing or
conflicting judgments are undecided. Preserve every dispatch; no favorable retry.

Personal adoption requires:

- complete generations and judgments;
- candidate preferred in at least four of six pairs, with no consistent incumbent win;
- author read-through finds no material meaning error in any candidate or unresolved
  obstacle requiring this intended reader to decode unexplained central terminology;
- all three native regressions pass (staged scope, exact JSON, submodule pointer);
- exact installation match and one native installed smoke pass.

This is a prespecified engineering acceptance rule for a personal tool, not a
statistical superiority test. Author review is subjective and not independent.
Label masking does not hide identifiable writing style. No human comprehension,
reading speed, vocabulary frequency corpus, or new user preference is measured.
A failed candidate is frozen; repairs need their own version and bounded protocol.
Maximum 28 calls: 12+12+3+1, no automatic retry. Install only after the first four
conditions; restore the backed-up incumbent if the installed smoke fails.

## Evidence

Pin all six skill resources in each arm plus cases, runner and this design before
live calls. Preserve raw responses, provider receipts and copied credentials only
outside Git; remove copied credentials after each run. Export hashes, actual model
identity and author findings. The reusable runners isolate native Codex and inject
Opus/Grok instructions inline; the latter do not prove native skill discovery.
